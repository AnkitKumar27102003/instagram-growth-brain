## 2. Strategic rationale

The recommendation connects a broad economic system—electricity tariffs—to an everyday concern: household bills. This makes the topic suitable for NAZAR's approach of explaining policy and economic mechanisms through their practical impact on people.

The strategy used a simulated Economy breakout signal as contextual input while selecting a fresh topic rather than repeating the breakout topic itself. The Conflict hook and Animated Explainer format were intended to give the subject a clear tension and a visual way to explain how tariff structures can affect what households pay.

This rationale is a concise reviewer-facing summary of the recorded recommendation and project behavior; it is not a verbatim copy of a full original Strategy Agent rationale.

## 3. Why this fits NAZAR

- **Facts:** The topic can be grounded in tariff structures, electricity pricing components, and relevant official sources.
- **Context:** The Reel can explain how the tariff system works rather than merely stating that bills rise or fall.
- **Perspective:** It links an economic or policy mechanism to household impact.
- **Accessible language:** Informative Hinglish is intended to make the explanation understandable to a general audience.
- **Visual storytelling:** An animated explainer can show how components of a bill relate to the tariff system.

## 4. Proposed narrative direction

1. **Hook — Conflict:** Introduce the tension between electricity being an everyday necessity and the way its tariff structure determines household costs.
2. **Explain the mechanism:** Break down, in simple terms, what a tariff is and which bill components the researched topic actually covers.
3. **Connect to households:** Show how the applicable tariff structure can affect a household bill, avoiding unsupported generalizations.
4. **Payoff:** Give viewers a clear takeaway about what to look for on an electricity bill or in the applicable tariff information.

This is a proposed narrative outline, not a recovered verbatim script from the original agent run.

## 5. Creative execution

- **Format:** Animated Explainer Reel, vertical 9:16.
- **Language:** Simple, natural Hinglish; explain technical terms.
- **Visual style:** Premium documentary/editorial look with restrained red and black accents, off-white paper textures, clear diagrams, and clean highlights.
- **Pacing:** Open immediately with the conflict; avoid a long channel introduction.
- **Evidence:** Use traceable sources for tariff rules, figures, and dates. Do not add a number unless it has been verified against the relevant source.
- **Editorial tone:** Clear, neutral, evidence-led, and practical.
